**The Last Train** — a night-train heist. You are a thief aboard a four-carriage sleeper train rolling through the night: luggage car, dining car, sleeper car, vault car. Guards patrol the aisles with sweeping vision cones, cameras sweep every carriage, and the alarm meter climbs whenever you are seen. Pocket the glowing safes and cases, crack the vault safe in the last car, then slip back to the rear door and jump to the getaway before the train reaches the terminus.

Routes matter: sneak through the carriages door to door, or climb the ladders at the carriage junctions and cross on the roof walkway — unseen, but far from the loot. Smoke pellets cost $1 of loot and blind every cone they cover; a thrown decoy coin lures the nearest guard away. The night slides past the windows in parallax — stars, moon, hills and telegraph poles — while the journey timer counts down to the terminus.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, W/S at a ladder to climb to the roof or drop back inside, E to crack the vault safe and to jump at the rear door, Q for a smoke pellet, F (or click) to throw a decoy coin. P pauses.
